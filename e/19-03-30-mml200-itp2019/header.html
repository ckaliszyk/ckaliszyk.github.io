This is the accompanying material for the paper:

  • Scalable Declarative Proof Translation

by C.Kaliszyk and K.Pąk submitted to ITP 2019

It contains an automated translation of 200 Mizar Mathematical Library articles with proof outlines translated to Isabelle/Isar together with the Isabelle/Mizar foundations which are required to load these 200 articles.

The formalizations are compatible with Isabelle 2018.
